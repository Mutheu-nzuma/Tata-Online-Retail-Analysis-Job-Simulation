# Tata Online Retail Analysis Job Simulation

This repository contains the files and analysis conducted for the Tata job simulation involving an online retail store's data.

**Project Goal:** To review the provided data, generate insightful analytics relevant to the CEO and CMO, and provide recommendations for business performance evaluation and future expansion.

**Problem Description:**

An online retail store had hired me as a consultant to analyze their sales data and provide actionable insights to the CEO and CMO. The management team is keen to understand the key drivers of their revenue, identify opportunities for strategic growth and expansion, and gain a better understanding of their customer base and market performance. They are particularly interested in viewing metrics from both an operational and marketing perspective and want to leverage demographic information (country in this case) to inform their decisions. The ultimate goal is to use data-driven insights to strategically plan for the next year and identify promising areas for business expansion.

**CEO Questions Answered by the Visualizations:**

    * **Visual:** Line Chart showing monthly revenue for 2011 (visuals/ceo\_revenue\_trend\_2011.png).

**CMO Questions Answered by the Visualizations:**

1.  **Which are the top 10 countries (excluding the United Kingdom) that are generating the highest revenue and the corresponding quantity sold?**
    * **Visual:** Dual-Axis Bar Chart comparing revenue and quantity sold for the top 10 countries (excluding the UK) (visuals/cmo\_top\_10\_countries\_revenue\_quantity.png).
    * **Insight Provided:** Identified key international markets with high revenue and sales volume.

2.  **Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?**
    * **Visual:** Horizontal Bar Chart showing the top 10 customers ranked by their total revenue contribution (visuals/cmo\_top\_10\_customers\_by\_revenue.png).
    * **Insight Provided:** Showed the most valuable customers and indicated a diversified revenue stream.

**Final Findings:**

* **Seasonality (CEO):** Revenue in 2011 exhibits a strong seasonal trend, with a significant surge starting in September and peaking in November. This suggests that the last quarter of the year is critical for revenue generation.
* **Top Growth Countries (CMO):** Excluding the UK, the Netherlands, Ireland, Germany, and France are the top countries in terms of both revenue and quantity sold, indicating strong existing markets with growth potential.
* **Top Customers (CMO):** The revenue generated by the top 10 customers is relatively well-diversified, indicating the business is not overly reliant on a small number of clients.
* **Global Demand (CEO):** Demand for products is currently concentrated in Europe and Australia, with minimal presence in the Americas, Africa, Asia (excluding Australia), and Russia, highlighting significant untapped market potential.

**Recommendations:**

* **Capitalize on Seasonality (CEO):** Further investigate the drivers behind the September-November revenue surge to optimize inventory, marketing campaigns, and resource allocation to maximize sales during this peak period in future years.
* **Focus on High-Performing Growth Markets (CEO & CMO):** Invest further in the Netherlands, Ireland, Germany, and France to solidify market share and explore strategies for continued growth in these regions. Analyze the marketing and sales tactics currently successful in these countries.
* **Explore Untapped Markets (CEO & CMO):** Conduct thorough market research in the Americas, Africa, Asia (excluding Australia), and Russia to understand the reasons for low demand. Develop tailored market entry strategies, considering product localization, pricing, and marketing approaches specific to these regions.
* **Nurture Top Customers (CMO):** While the customer base is diversified, implement strategies to enhance engagement and loyalty among the top revenue-generating customers, such as personalized offers or loyalty programs.
* **Further Analysis (CEO & CMO):** Conduct deeper analysis into customer segmentation, product affinities, and repeat purchase behavior to refine marketing strategies and product offerings.
